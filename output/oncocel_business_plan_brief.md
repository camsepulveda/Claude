## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

OncoCel LLC is positioning to become Puerto Rico's first vertically integrated CAR-T cellular therapy company. The strategy is built on a **three-pillar revenue model** — general oncology, autologous stem cell transplant (SCT), and CAR-T — designed to mitigate financial risk during the CAR-T ramp while building toward VC readiness by 2028-2029.

OncoCel addresses a critical structural gap: **Puerto Rico has no local CAR-T or SCT program capable of retaining patients on-island.** The territory's 3.2 million residents with hematologic malignancies must currently travel to mainland US centers at significant personal and financial cost — a burden that falls disproportionately on a population where nearly 50% depend on Medicaid.

### 2.2 The Structural Access Gap

**Puerto Rico has no CAR-T program. Most transplant-eligible patients leave the island.**

- All CAR-T eligible patients must travel to **mainland US** — MD Anderson, Moffitt, Memorial Sloan Kettering, etc.
- Most auto SCT-eligible patients also travel to the mainland due to limited local capacity
- Average patient travel burden: **$15,000-$30,000** (flights, 4-6 weeks lodging, caregiver costs)
- Nearly **50% of PR population** covered by Medicaid — PR Medicaid generally does not reimburse mainland care
- Nationally only **2 in 10** eligible patients receive CAR-T; in Puerto Rico estimated **<1 in 10**
- Post-hurricane (2017) infrastructure and economic challenges continue to compound access barriers
- Healthcare access historically limited — specialized care often requires travel to San Juan or Fajardo even within the island

### 2.3 Existing Infrastructure (OncoCel Advantage)

- **Hospital Auxilio Mutuo (HAM):** BMT program since 2015; allo-HSCT since 2017; 6-room transplant unit. HAM + Red Cross apheresis SLA under negotiation covers both SCT and CAR-T collections — one SLA serves both OncoCel programs
- **OncoCel-HAM relationship:** Already collaborating on CIBMTR data reporting (since 2023). Natural referral pipeline and clinical partnership
- **HIMAHEALTH:** Building comprehensive stem cell/BMT center — validates market demand but is not pursuing CAR-T
- **Roswell Park Cancer Institute:** OncoCel's GMP manufacturing partner. MOU for CAR-T manufacturing + Puerto Rican cohort recruitment. Critical bridge until on-island manufacturing established

### 2.4 Regulatory Tailwinds (2025-2026)

| Development | Date | Impact for OncoCel |
|-------------|------|--------------------|
| FDA removes REMS for all 7 autologous CAR-T products | Jun 2025 | Eliminates mandatory hospital certification; opens ambulatory/community path |
| FACT Community Clinical Setting Standards (1st Edition) | Nov 2025 | First accreditation framework designed for community/ambulatory CAR-T programs |
| CMS Cell & Gene Therapy Access Model launched | 2025-2026 | Puerto Rico is a participating territory; outcomes-based Medicaid coverage for cell/gene therapies |
| Medicare CAR-T base payment increase (MS-DRG 018) | FY2026 | $314,176 (up from $269,139 in FY2025); NTAP cap raised to 65% |
| Gilead anito-cel (outpatient-designed CAR-T) | Est. 2026 | First CAR-T designed specifically for outpatient administration |

### 2.5 Why Puerto Rico, Why Now

- **US territory** = FDA jurisdiction, Medicare/Medicaid eligible, US pharma supply chain
- **Act 60 tax incentives** for healthcare services and R&D organizations — directly lowers OncoCel operating costs
- **AdvanCel R&D Decree** (unused, potentially transferable) — saves 12-18 months vs. new DDEC application; immediate R&D legitimacy
- **Geographic hub** for Caribbean/LatAm medical tourism (DR, USVI, BVI, Trinidad & Tobago)
- **Lower operating costs** vs. mainland with US-standard reimbursement rates
- **Bilingual workforce** — serves 100% of PR population; high pharma interest for Hispanic/Latino clinical trial enrollment (underrepresented population)
- **First-mover timing** — REMS removal + FACT community standards create a narrow window before competitors enter
