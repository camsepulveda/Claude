# ONCOCEL AMBULATORY CENTER
## Business Plan Brief — TCT Oncology
### Target Launch: October 1, 2026

---

## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Oncocel** is a first-of-its-kind ambulatory CAR T-cell therapy and cellular therapy center in Puerto Rico, operated by TCT Oncology. By bringing FDA-approved CAR T-cell therapies to the island, Oncocel eliminates the need for patients to travel to the US mainland for treatment — reducing costs by ~40%, improving outcomes through continuity of care, and addressing a critical healthcare access gap for 3.2 million residents.

**Opening Day:** October 1, 2026
**Location:** San Juan Metro Area, Puerto Rico
**Model:** Outpatient/ambulatory CAR T-cell therapy administration with short-stay observation
**Regulatory Context:** FDA removed REMS for all 7 approved CAR-T products (June 2025); FACT Community Clinical Setting Standards released (November 2025) — both directly enabling this center model.

### 2.2 The Access Gap

**There is currently no CAR T-cell therapy center in Puerto Rico.**

- Eligible patients must travel to **mainland US** centers (MD Anderson, Moffitt, Memorial Sloan Kettering, etc.)
- Average travel cost per patient: **$15,000-$30,000** (flights, 4-6 weeks lodging, caregiver expenses)
- Nearly **50% of Puerto Rico's population** is on Medicaid — Puerto Rico Medicaid generally does not cover mainland care
- Nationally, only **2 in 10** eligible patients receive CAR-T; in Puerto Rico the ratio is estimated at **<1 in 10**
- Post-hurricane infrastructure and economic challenges compound access barriers

### 2.3 Existing Infrastructure (Advantage for Oncocel)

- **Hospital Auxilio Mutuo (HAM):** BMT program operational since 2015; allo-HSCT since 2017; already collaborates with TCT Oncology on CIBMTR reporting — provides natural referral pipeline and clinical partnership
- **HIMAHEALTH:** Building comprehensive stem cell/BMT center — potential competitor but validates market demand
- **TCT Oncology's track record** in Puerto Rico hematology positions Oncocel as the logical next step from BMT to cellular therapy

### 2.4 Regulatory Tailwinds

| Development | Date | Impact for Oncocel |
|-------------|------|--------------------|
| FDA removes REMS for all CAR-T products | Jun 2025 | Eliminates mandatory hospital certification; opens ambulatory path |
| FACT Community Clinical Setting Standards | Nov 2025 | First accreditation framework designed for ambulatory CAR-T |
| CMS Cell & Gene Therapy Access Model | 2025-2026 | Puerto Rico is a participating territory; outcomes-based Medicaid coverage |
| Medicare CAR-T Base Payment increase | FY2026 | $314,176 (up from $269,139); improved reimbursement economics |
| Gilead anito-cel (outpatient CAR-T) | Est. 2026 | First CAR-T designed for outpatient-only administration |

### 2.5 Puerto Rico as Strategic Beachhead

- US territory = FDA jurisdiction, Medicare/Medicaid eligible, US pharmaceutical supply chain
- Act 60 tax incentives for healthcare services and research organizations
- Geographic hub for Caribbean and Latin American medical tourism (Dominican Republic, USVI, BVI)
- Lower operating costs vs. mainland (facilities, labor) with US-standard reimbursement
- Bilingual (Spanish/English) workforce addressing growing Hispanic patient population nationwide

## V. COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES

1. **First mover in Puerto Rico** — No competing CAR-T center on the island
2. **TCT Oncology's existing hematology presence** — Established referral relationships and CIBMTR reporting with HAM
3. **Ambulatory model** — 40% cost reduction vs. inpatient; aligns with FDA's post-REMS direction and payer preferences
4. **FACT Community Standards** — Purpose-built for this exact center model (released Nov 2025)
5. **CMS CGT Access Model** — Puerto Rico's participation enables Medicaid coverage pathway
6. **Bilingual capability** — Serves 100% of PR population; positions for LatAm medical tourism
7. **Act 60 incentives** — Tax advantages lower operating costs vs. mainland competitors
8. **Outpatient focus** — Gilead's 2026 anito-cel launch designed specifically for outpatient; Oncocel will be day-one ready
9. **Research site value** — Underrepresented Hispanic/Latino population = high pharma interest for diverse trial enrollment
