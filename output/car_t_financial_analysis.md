| Carvykti (Legend/J&J) | ~$465,000 |

### Total Cost of Care
- **Median total cost**: $500,000 - $1,000,000+ per patient
- **Hospital recommendation**: Bill 4-5x drug price ($2M-$2.5M) to avoid losses
- **Patient out-of-pocket**: Median $5,547 (range up to $29,000)
- **Nearly 50%** of patients report moderate-to-severe financial toxicity

### Medicare Reimbursement
- **MS-DRG 018 Base Payment FY2025**: $269,139
- **MS-DRG 018 Base Payment FY2026**: $314,176 (increase)
- **NTAP Cap**: 65% (up from 50%)
- **Gap**: Reimbursement still falls short of drug acquisition + care costs
- **Outpatient**: Reimbursed at ASP + 6% (generally more adequate)

### Reimbursement Challenge
- Only **2 in 10** eligible DLBCL patients actually receive CAR-T
- Medicare/Medicaid patients face greatest access barriers
- Commercial payer reimbursement generally offsets Medicare losses

## 4. Puerto Rico Considerations

- Puerto Rico operates under US FDA regulatory framework
- Medicare/Medicaid coverage applies but with territorial adjustments
- Limited number of CAR-T authorized treatment centers
- Patient access constrained by:
  - Travel requirements to mainland US centers
  - Limited local manufacturing/logistics infrastructure
  - Territorial Medicaid funding caps
- Potential for clinical trial site expansion

## 7. Emerging Trends & Future Outlook

### In Vivo CAR-T
- Market valued at $368.1M (2024), projected to reach **$44.2B by 2034** (61.5% CAGR)
- Eliminates need for ex vivo manufacturing
- AbbVie's $2.1B Capstan acquisition signals Big Pharma conviction

### Allogeneic (Off-the-Shelf) CAR-T
- Could dramatically reduce costs and manufacturing time
- 2026 is the "defining clinical test" year
- Caribou, Allogene, CRISPR Therapeutics leading development

### Autoimmune Disease Applications
- BMS CD19 NEX-T in Phase 1 for lupus, systemic sclerosis, myopathies
- 94% of evaluable patients off chronic immunosuppression
- Cabaletta Bio's CABA-201 showing promise

### Solid Tumor Expansion
- Currently all approved products target blood cancers
- Active trials for glioblastoma, ovarian, lung cancer
- Represents the next major growth frontier

### Cost Reduction Pathways
- Manufacturing automation (Kite quadrupling output by 2026)
- Outpatient administration trend
- Allogeneic "off-the-shelf" approaches
- CMS considering value-based/bundled payment models for 2026+

## 8. Key Challenges

1. **Cost Barrier**: $373K-$475K drug + $500K-$1M total care
2. **Reimbursement Gap**: Medicare underpays by $100K-$300K per case
3. **Manufacturing Complexity**: 3-4 week turnaround, patient-specific
4. **Safety Concerns**: CRS, neurotoxicity, secondary malignancies
5. **Access Inequality**: Only 20% of eligible patients treated
6. **Geographic Limitations**: Concentrated in major academic centers
7. **Regulatory Scrutiny**: FDA probing T-cell malignancy risk
