# ONCOCEL LLC — Executive Summary

**Puerto Rico's First Vertically Integrated CAR-T Cellular Therapy Company**
Confidential | June 2026

---

## The Problem

Puerto Rico has **no CAR-T or autologous stem cell transplant (SCT) program** capable of retaining patients on-island. For a territory of 3.2 million people generating ~1,000 new hematologic malignancies per year, every eligible patient must fly to the US mainland for treatment — at $15,000-$30,000 in personal cost per case. Nearly 50% of Puerto Rico's population depends on Medicaid, which generally does not reimburse mainland care. The result: **fewer than 1 in 10 eligible patients in Puerto Rico receive CAR-T therapy**, compared to 2 in 10 nationally.

## The Solution

OncoCel LLC is a vertically integrated ambulatory cellular therapy company built on a **three-pillar revenue model** that mitigates financial risk during the CAR-T ramp:

| Pillar | Launch | Revenue/Case | Strategic Role |
|--------|--------|-------------|----------------|
| General Oncology | Oct 2026 | Per-visit | Immediate cash flow; identifies SCT/CAR-T patients |
| Autologous SCT | Nov 2026 | $150-200K | Financial bridge; validates infrastructure; advances FACT |
| CAR-T Cellular Therapy | Q1 2027 | $400-500K | VC anchor; research-first (IRB), selective commercial |

Infrastructure overlap between SCT and CAR-T is **~80%** — apheresis, cryopreservation, infusion suite, nursing, SOPs, and FACT accreditation all serve both programs. SCT activates everything CAR-T needs.

## Market Opportunity

The US CAR-T market reached **$6.3 billion in 2025** (70% of global) and is projected to grow at 30.4% CAGR to **$42.6 billion by 2035**. The FDA removed REMS for all 7 approved CAR-T products (June 2025) and FACT released Community Clinical Setting Standards (November 2025) — both directly enabling OncoCel's ambulatory model. Puerto Rico joined the CMS Cell & Gene Therapy Access Model, creating a Medicaid coverage pathway. Outpatient CAR-T administration saves **40% vs. inpatient** settings.
